Triple Pendulum Lab

Agent

Be realistic. A DQN trained from scratch usually won't master three links in a few minutes; without demonstrations it mostly learns to delay the fall. Demonstration seeding helps, but results vary a lot from run to run: some runs stand within a few minutes of compute, others are still wobbling after ten. Physics

Equations of motion are derived with Lagrangian mechanics and integrated with RK4 at 5 ms sub-steps; the agent acts at 50 Hz.
